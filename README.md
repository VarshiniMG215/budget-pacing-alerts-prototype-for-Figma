# Budget Pacing Alerts for Ad Campaigns

A product case study I did while preparing for product roles. It's about a problem advertisers run into on ad platforms: they find out too late that a campaign is overspending or underspending.

**Live prototype:** https://varshinimg215.github.io/budget-pacing-alerts-prototype/

## The problem
Advertisers set a daily budget, but nothing tells them mid-day if the campaign is on pace. A campaign can use up its whole budget by 3 PM and miss the best hours, or barely spend and waste the day. Either way, ROAS suffers.

## Who it's for
- Performance marketers who manage several campaigns a day
- Agency teams who need to watch many accounts at once
- Small business owners who run ads themselves and can't check all day

## What I proposed
1. **Pacing status on the campaign list:** On track, Overspending or Underspending, visible at a glance.
2. **Planned vs. actual spend chart** on the campaign page, with a projected time the budget will run out.
3. **Alert rules:** the user picks the threshold (for example 80% of budget used) and where to get alerts (in app, email, WhatsApp).
4. **One-click actions in the alert:** increase budget, pause, or ignore.

## How I prioritised
I scored each feature with RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort). The pacing status and the alert rule came first because they reach every user and take the least effort. WhatsApp alerts and copying rules across campaigns are later phases.

## Success metrics
- Share of campaigns that finish the day within 10% of their budget
- Time between an alert and the advertiser's action
- Alert-to-action rate (how many alerts lead to a change)
